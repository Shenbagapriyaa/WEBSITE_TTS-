The Track Tech / Trackwell workflow

Follow an order from the first roll of fabric to shipment

Everyone sees the same journey: materials, orders, work in progress, production updates and quality checks, all in one place.

  1. 01

    Material Receiving

    Fabric and other materials are checked in as they arrive.

  2. 02

    GRN

    The team records what came in and confirms the quantities.

  3. 03

    Fabric Inspection & Store

    Each fabric roll is checked before it is put away in the store.

  4. 04

    Relaxation

    The fabric rests and settles properly before cutting.

  5. 05

    Reservation & Issue

    The right fabric is set aside for the order and issued to production.

  6. 06

    Order Creation SAP → Trackwell

    Order details from SAP, including quantity, size ratio and delivery date, are created in Trackwell.

  7. 07

    Pattern & Marker

    The CAD team prepares the pattern and lays out the marker for cutting.

  8. 08

    Cutting

    The marker guides the cut, including end bits and any recutting needed.

  9. 09

    Bundle / QR Tracking

    Cut pieces are bundled, scanned and followed as they move to the next stage.

  10. 10

    Sewing / Production Inline → Mid → End

    Teams update output and defects as the bundle moves through each sewing stage.

  11. 11

    Washing / Laser

    Garments go through the wash or laser process the buyer has requested.

  12. 12

    Finishing

    Trimming, ironing, measurement and final inspection happen here, along with any rework.

  13. 13

    Packing

    Finished pieces are folded, bagged, tagged and packed by order or buyer.

  14. 14

    Shipment

    The order is ready to leave the factory and head to the customer.
